Code lines split into implementation, test, doc and comment.

With --by file, JSON groups also carry the file's non-test fn metrics (see
`bloaty metrics`): `largest_fn` {name, line, lines},
`max_cognitive_complexity`, and `max_cyclomatic_complexity` and
`max_nesting` (reported, not scored) {name, line, value}, `most_panics` {name, line, value, sites}
(the fn with the most panic sites and where they are, as in `bloaty metrics`;
absent without panics), `panics`, `clones`, and `panic_density` (panic sites
per 100 impl lines, only from 30 impl lines). Fn fields are absent without
fns; all are absent for whole-test files.

They also carry `smell` (files with >= 10 impl lines and a fn, not
whole-test, build script or example), ranked against every such file in the
linked workspace, so PATH doesn't change it: `size`, `complexity` and
`fragility` as percentiles (largest fn or impl lines; cognitive
complexity; panic density of files with >= 2 panics,
>= 30 impl lines and >= 1 per 100, fixed bands 0.9/0.75/0.5 at >= 3/1.5/1
per 100 when under 5 files qualify; a zero signal is 0), `front` (Pareto
front on those three, 1 = nothing is worse on all) and `level`: "hot" for
the first files walking the fronts with a dimension >= 0.9 (at most 5% of
files), "warm" for the next by sum with one >= 0.75 (at most 10% more,
none under 20 files), "noted" (below), else null. Hot and warm need a fn
of >= 40 lines, cognitive complexity >= 15 or the panic density above,
and a culprit among the signals that level would name (`culprit` below):
a file with nothing to point at gets no level (it is still ranked, and
its slot goes to the next file). Hot also needs a culprit that isn't a
long flat fn (`flat` below: >= 60 lines with nothing nested: no cognitive
increment over 1): a file whose only culprits are long flat fns is warm
at most. A longer flat fn doesn't hide a culprit-sized fn with something
nested: both are named.

Excused signals don't count toward the score (see `bloaty metrics` for
`shape` and `allowed`):
- a table's literal: its lines (the rest of the fn counts as usual);
- a dispatch's small arms (<= 12 code lines and cognitive complexity
  <= 3 in the body, the pattern aside; each measured like a fn, from
  nesting 0): it counts as its remainder (the lets before the match, the
  scrutinee) plus every arm that isn't small: their lines added, and the
  match's 1 plus their cognitive complexity added to the remainder's;
- a fn's lines under #[allow(clippy::too_many_lines)], its cognitive
  complexity under #[allow(clippy::cognitive_complexity)]. Also #[expect],
  and inside #[cfg_attr(..)] unless its condition is `any()`; only on the
  fn itself, not its impl or module, and not via clippy::all or
  clippy::pedantic. Below twice the bar only: from 80 lines or cognitive
  complexity 30 the allow is ignored.
Panics never are. "noted" files would have been hot or warm with them
counted, and an excused fn whose own number reaches the culprit bar is
behind that (only such excused signals are listed). A dispatch
arm of >= 60 lines or cognitive complexity >= 10 keeps a file that the
whole dispatch would make hot or warm at the worse of both levels,
pointing at the arm.

Levelled files have `why`: the signals behind the level with raw numbers,
as text for display, and `dims`: the same structured, one entry per
signal [{dim, metric, value, name?, line?, arm?, panics?, culprit?,
flat?, excused?}].
`dim` is size/complexity/fragility; `metric` is largest_fn (value: its
lines; name, line: the fn), impl_lines (value: the file's),
cognitive_complexity (value; name, line: the fn), or panic_density
(value: per 100 impl lines, a float; panics: the count).
`arm` {name, line, value}: a dispatch's arm to blame (its worst counted
arm outweighs the rest of the fn), with the arm's own number. `culprit: true` marks real signals worth pointing
at: a fn of >= 60 lines or cognitive complexity >= 10, and the panic
density; every hot or warm file has one. `flat: true` marks a culprit
largest_fn whose fn (or blamed arm) has nothing nested, as the score
counts it (a dispatch: its remainder and counted arms, each from inside
the arm): long but flat, worth a look rather than a split. `excused`
marks what the file is noted for (hot and warm files can have such entries
too), with why: {kind: "table", entries}, {kind: "dispatch", arms,
repeat?} or {kind: "allowed", lint: "too_many_lines" |
"cognitive_complexity"}; `repeat` {statements, count, at: [line, ...]}:
a shared prefix: `count` arms open with the same `statements` statements (names and
literals aside; at least 12 tokens with a call or control flow), `at`
where the first 20 copies start.
Text marks hot !!, warm ! and noted ~.
